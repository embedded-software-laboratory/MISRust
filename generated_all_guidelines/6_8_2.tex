\textbf{Analysis Result} \\
(C3) Rule can be dropped completely \\

\textbf{Comment:} Rust requires a lifetime annotation for returned references (which can never be valid for local variables), and additionally prohibits return of references to local variables. Returning a dangling pointer is safe to do in Rust, but results in a warning. Dereferencing the pointer is unsafe and will cause UB (see example). The warning can be promoted to a compilation error. \\
