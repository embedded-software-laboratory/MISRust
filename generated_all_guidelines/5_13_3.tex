\textbf{Comment:} The rationale for the rule is to avoid confusion, as in \Cpp a leading \texttt{0} marks an octal constant. In Rust, the leading \texttt{0} is ignored. To create an octal value, the \texttt{0o} prefix is required, leaving no room for confusion. \\
