\paragraph{Rule 9.4.2}
\begin{longtable}{p{13cm}}
\textbf{Analysis Result} \\
(C3) Rule can be dropped completely \\
\end{longtable}
\textbf{Comment:} Rust has no \texttt{switch} statement, the closest equivalent could be a \texttt{match} expression. The rules ensure the complex structure of a switch statement is ``well-formed''. Rust \texttt{match} statements are required to be exhaustive and have clear pattern matching syntax using logical boolean operators, removing all of the ambiguity. \\
