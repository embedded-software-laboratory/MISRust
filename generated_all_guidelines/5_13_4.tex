\textbf{Analysis Result} \\
(C3) Rule can be dropped completely \\

\textbf{Comment:} Rust supports explicit suffixing of unsigned integer literals to specify their type. Common suffixes include \texttt{u8}, \texttt{u16}, \texttt{u32}, \texttt{u64}, \texttt{u128}, and \texttt{usize}. However, Rust does not have automatic integer promotion and its type system is platform-consistent. The rationale of this rule does not apply anymore. \\
