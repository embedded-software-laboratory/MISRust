\textbf{Analysis Result} \\
(C3) Rule can be dropped completely \\

\textbf{Comment:} An array is a fixed-size sequence in Rust, passing as a reference is possible. In contrast to \Cpp, the reference is a \textit{slice} type, not a raw pointer, and bounds are still checked during compilation and runtime `Rust Reference: Array types, Slice types p. 279/432ff' (see example). Arrays passed over FFI boundaries are out of scope. \\
